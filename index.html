Keeping houseplants alive in winter

Gardening · 6 min read

Shorter days change what houseplants need.

Most of them slow down in winter, so they drink less and stop putting out new leaves.

Check the soil before you water. Push a finger about two centimeters into the pot. If it's still damp, wait a few days.

Each setting comes from a published rule. Clear it with room to spare and nothing changes.

  • Text size

    Text height at least twice the smallest E you can read.

    Legge and Bigelow, 2011
  • Contrast

    Text contrast at least ten times the faintest E you see, never below four.

    Whittaker and Lovie-Kitchin, 1994
  • Color

    Your limits in three color directions, against reference limits.

    Cambridge Colour Test
  • Night

    Dark mode only if your limit there is better by 0.15 log units.

    Mars chart retest range

Questions

About the tests, your data and the extension.

Is EyeScore an eye exam?

No. It finds settings for this screen and can't detect eye conditions.

Where does EyeScore keep my results?

In your browser, on your own computer. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there's no account.

Does EyeScore cost anything?

No. The tests and the extension are free.

Will EyeScore break the websites I use?

It keeps each site's desktop layout and checks it after enlarging. You can turn it off on any site.

Why does EyeScore need full brightness?

Your limits depend on the screen, so the same brightness and distance keep results steady.
